## Research integrity
- Do not invent customer quotes, interview counts, benchmarks, or market statistics.
- Record contradictory evidence, not only supporting evidence.
- Use exact source links and publication dates.
- Separate facts from interpretation.
- An empty log means research is pending, not that assumptions are validated.
