## Outreach messages

### Connection request (short)

Hi [Name] — I’m researching how LinkedIn personal-branding teams manage client delivery behind the scenes: research, drafts, feedback, publishing, and reporting. I’m speaking with practitioners to understand the real workflow, not pitching a tool. Would love to connect.

### After connection

Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I’m mapping how independent ghostwriters and small personal-branding agencies actually deliver client work, especially the steps that create the most revisions or coordination. Could I ask you about one recent client workflow in a 15–20 minute conversation? No pitch, and I’m happy to keep details anonymous.

### Warm introduction

Hey [Name], I’m exploring how LinkedIn personal-branding services are delivered day to day and want to learn from people doing the work. Would you be comfortable introducing me to one freelancer or agency operator who handles client work every week? I’m only asking for a short research conversation, not selling anything.

### Follow-up (send once, after a reasonable interval)

Hi [Name] — just resurfacing this in case it got buried. I’m trying to understand the real operating process behind client content delivery, and even 15 minutes would help. No worries if the timing isn’t right.

Personalize the message to a person's actual work. Do not send repeated follow-ups or use automation to spam prospects.

## Qualification questions before scheduling

1. Have they personally delivered LinkedIn/personal-branding work for paying clients in the last 30 days?
2. Do they handle the workflow themselves or manage people who do?
3. Can they describe a recent concrete case, not only their general philosophy?
4. Are they a buyer, an operator, or both?
5. Are they comfortable sharing a sanitized example without client-confidential information?

If someone is only a creator managing their own account, classify them as a separate segment. Do not mix their evidence with agency delivery without noting the distinction.

- Keep customer names, client materials, and sensitive details out of public notes unless explicit permission is granted.
- A first set of 3–5 interviews is a learning loop, not proof of market size.
